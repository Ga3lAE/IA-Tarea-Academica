# Sección 11: Referencias Bibliográficas

A continuación se listan las publicaciones científicas y referencias metodológicas citadas en el informe, estructuradas bajo la norma oficial **IEEE**:

1. E. Aiken, S. Bellue, D. Karlan, C. Udry, and J. Blumenstock, "Machine learning and phone data can improve targeting of humanitarian aid," *Nature*, vol. 603, no. 7903, pp. 864--870, 2022.

3. L. McBride and A. Nichols, "Retooling poverty targeting using out-of-sample machine learning and proxy means tests," *The World Bank Economic Review*, vol. 32, no. 3, pp. 531--550, 2018.
4. World Bank and EAAMO, "Moving targets: When does a poverty prediction model need to be updated?," in *Proceedings of the 3rd ACM Conference on Equity and Access in Algorithms, Mechanisms, and Optimization (EAAMO)*, pp. 1--12, 2023.
5. G. Ke, Q. Meng, T. Finley, T. Wang, W. Chen, W. Ma, Q. Ye, and T.-Y. Liu, "LightGBM: A highly efficient gradient boosting decision tree," in *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS)*, vol. 30, pp. 3146--3154, 2017.
6. S. M. Lundberg and S.-I. Lee, "A unified approach to interpreting model predictions," in *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS)*, vol. 30, pp. 4765--4774, 2017.
7. A. Fernández, S. García, F. Herrera, and N. V. Chawla, "SMOTE for learning from imbalanced data: progress and challenges, marking the 15-year anniversary," *Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research*, vol. 61, pp. 863--905, 2018.
8. V. O. K. Li, "Hints on writing technical papers and making presentations," *IEEE Transactions on Education*, vol. 42, no. 2, pp. 134--137, mayo de 1999.
